### Summary
Researchers can save dated place-condition audits independently of respondent opinions.

### Why this is needed
Capture structured field observations separately from interviews enables the field survey and gps collection workstream while preserving the researcher-led evidence chain. Without this deliverable: observation without a participant never creates a survey respondent or invented consent is not established or verified.

### User story
As a field researcher, I need this reviewed capability so that researchers can save dated place-condition audits independently of respondent opinions.

### Scope
- Researchers can save dated place-condition audits independently of respondent opinions.
- Implement/review the associated contract and observable error states described in `docs/research/survey-questionnaire.md`.
- Capture evidence for the negative case: Observation without a participant never creates a survey respondent or invented consent.

### Out of scope
Other backlog issue deliverables, unrelated refactors, real data committed to Git, and unapproved paid-provider activation. Planning/design/research issues produce reviewed artifacts rather than application code; implementation issues build only the stated capability.
