### Summary
Fresh capture stores reported accuracy/time/method and permission/error status.

### Why this is needed
Capture mobile geolocation and permission state enables the field survey and gps collection workstream while preserving the researcher-led evidence chain. Without this deliverable: no fabricated coordinate after denied or failed capture is not established or verified.

### User story
As a field researcher, I need this reviewed capability so that fresh capture stores reported accuracy/time/method and permission/error status.
