### Summary
Branching, refusal/unsure and per-subject responses validate consistently.

### Why this is needed
Render and validate versioned questions enables the field survey and gps collection workstream while preserving the researcher-led evidence chain. Without this deliverable: inactive branch or mismatched form answer not counted is not established or verified.

### User story
As a field researcher, I need this reviewed capability so that branching, refusal/unsure and per-subject responses validate consistently.

### Scope
- Branching, refusal/unsure and per-subject responses validate consistently.
- Implement/review the associated contract and observable error states described in `docs/research/survey-questionnaire.md`.
- Capture evidence for the negative case: Inactive branch or mismatched form answer not counted.
