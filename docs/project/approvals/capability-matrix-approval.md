## Stakeholder Decisions
1. **Lead and Reviewer Distinction:** Yes, lead and reviewer are distinct roles. A researcher cannot approve their own collected content for publication; publication requires an independent reviewer.
2. **Restricted Exports:** Restricted research exports are limited to lead and administrator roles, strictly purpose-bound with audit logging.
3. **Break-Glass Emergency Access:** Yes, permitted under emergency break-glass conditions for administrators/leads with mandatory reason, maximum 4 hours duration, and immutable audit log.
4. **MFA Roles:** Multi-factor authentication is required for `lead`, `reviewer`, and `administrator`.
5. **Administrator Content Isolation:** Administrator has account and operational management grants but has no default access to private interview content or raw coordinates.
