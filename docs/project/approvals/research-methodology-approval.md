## Research Questions & Methodology Sign-off
1. **Evidence Classification & Separation:**
   - Four distinct evidence classes are enforced:
     1. Initial researcher observation (date/rubric/observer)
     2. Respondent account (instrument/topic/consent)
     3. Historical claim (citation/date/quality)
     4. Verified fact (independent reviewer/supporting evidence)
   - Respondent opinions are strictly labeled as respondent perspectives and cannot be relabeled or aggregated as measured population facts.

2. **Sampling Limitations & Rigor:**
   - Purposive convenience sampling limitations are recorded in `docs/research/methodology.md` and `docs/research/sampling-plan.md`.
   - Ratings and responses represent the sampled participants only; general population causal claims or decorative confidence intervals are prohibited.
